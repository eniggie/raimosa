# RAIMOSA AI — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 7, 2026
**Publisher:** ECONTEUR LLC

## The short version

RAIMOSA AI collects nothing, transmits nothing, and has no servers. It runs
entirely on your computer.

## What data RAIMOSA accesses

RAIMOSA is a local desktop tool. To do its job it reads and writes files, and
inspects your device, **only within the scope you approve inside the app** —
for example, a folder you select, or a specific action you confirm. This
happens on your machine and nowhere else.

## What RAIMOSA sends off your device

Nothing. RAIMOSA has no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no
accounts, and no external model provider. Its network access is limited to:

- **Loopback (this computer only):** the interface talks to a local engine at
  `127.0.0.1`. These requests never leave your machine.
- **Your local network, only if you choose to:** you can pair a phone as a
  remote. That connection stays on your local network and requires a session
  you start and can revoke at any moment.

RAIMOSA does not connect to the internet to function.

## Where your data is stored

On your computer, in your user profile:

- A **receipt ledger** and **authority state** (record of what tools ran and
  what access was granted). Credentials are stored only as one-way hashes,
  never in a form that could be reused. File **contents are never stored** in
  the ledger — only paths, sizes, and cryptographic hashes.
- A **workspace folder** you choose for RAIMOSA's file operations.

You can read, export, or delete this data yourself at any time. Uninstalling
the app and removing that folder removes everything.

## Children's privacy

RAIMOSA is a general-purpose utility, collects no personal information, and is
not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
